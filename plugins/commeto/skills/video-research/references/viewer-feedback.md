# Viewer feedback and practical value

Use comments to assess whether viewers learned something or obtained a useful
result. This applies to all learning topics, including practical demonstrations.
Comments are a major value signal, not factual verification or a popularity vote.

## What to examine

When accessible, sample up to 20 substantive top comments and up to 20 recent
comments, deduplicated. Use what the host exposes; this is a target, not permission
to bypass restrictions or a reason to invent a sample. Include relevant replies
that clarify a correction. If only one ordering or a smaller sample is available,
record it. Never claim the sample represents all viewers.

Give more weight to specific first-hand outcomes, explanations of what became
clear, successful attempts, missing steps, reproducible problems, and well-supported
corrections. Give little weight to generic praise, likes, repeated text, promotion,
or unrelated discussion. Do not assume a comment is genuine or diagnose bots from
tone alone. Check allegations and technical corrections before treating them as facts.

Record the actual number of comments read, ordering when known, date inspected,
recurring benefits and problems, and links to comments when available (otherwise
link the video and label it as the sample source). Summarize rather than reproduce
whole comments or collect unnecessary personal information. A search snippet or
publisher description is not an inspected comment thread.

## Report viewer value

- **Strong:** multiple distinct, substantive reports of learning or successful use,
  without a recurring unresolved problem in the inspected sample.
- **Mixed:** substantive positive reports alongside recurring limitations or problems.
- **Limited:** inspected feedback offers little specific evidence of learning or results.
- **Unknown:** comments were unavailable or too few substantive comments were accessible.

These are qualitative judgments of the inspected sample, not numerical ratings,
truth probabilities, or evidence that an entire audience agrees. State the sample
size and limits alongside the label. Absence of comments is not evidence of poor
quality. Keep usefulness and trustworthiness separate; never let praise override a
contradicted core claim. If the user requires positive comment evidence, do not
silently substitute videos whose comments could not be inspected.

The optional evaluate.py helper computes credibility only. It does not fetch,
validate, or score comments; viewer-value findings are reported separately.
