## Submission procedure

1. Sign in to https://platform.openai.com/plugins using the intended organization.
2. Confirm individual or business verification and publishing permissions.
3. Upload `commeto-1.1.0.zip` from the release assets.
4. Use the skills-only route; no MCP server or credentials are needed.
5. Inspect imported listing text, icon, links, and skills.
6. Resolve automated findings, submit for review, and publish after approval.

Any agreement presented by the platform needs the publisher's acceptance.
Submission and approval are not guaranteed by a passing local build.
